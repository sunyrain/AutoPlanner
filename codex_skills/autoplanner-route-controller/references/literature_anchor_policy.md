# Literature Anchor Policy

Literature can support planning, not final closure.

Accepted anchor types:
- Exact-target or exact-intermediate synthesis evidence.
- Close analog or family precedent with explicit limitation labels.
- Template or disconnection evidence that can guide a later deterministic search.

Anchor-only outcomes must be `partial_anchor_only_not_solved` unless an executable route with atom-accounting and stock closure passes deterministic audit.

Do not use biological activity, assay, pharmacology, or toxicity papers as route anchors unless they contain route-relevant synthesis evidence.
