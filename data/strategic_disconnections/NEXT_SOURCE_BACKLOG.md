## Next High-Value Families

templates:

- `condition_envelope_cross_step_compatibility_policy`: multistep condition
  compatibility, pH/temperature/solvent carryover, aqueous/organic handoff, and
  protecting-group or enzyme-context records where route steps cannot be judged
  independently.
- `reagent_role_and_atom_contribution_semantics_policy`: reactant/reagent/catalyst
  role assignment, carrier-reagent atom contribution, sacrificial scaffolds, and
  atom-provenance records where precursor roles change route meaning.
- `route_visual_scheme_to_graph_alignment_policy`: rendered scheme, text table,
  reaction graph, SMILES, atom map, and figure provenance records where visual
  route evidence must be reconciled with machine-readable data.
- `expert_review_calibration_inter_rater_policy`: reviewer rubric, inter-rater
  agreement, disagreement adjudication, label drift, and calibration examples for
  route-quality source labels.
- `negative_control_decoy_route_generation_policy`: synthetic hard negatives,
  decoy routes, corrupted routes, self-loop artifacts, and benchmark controls
  used to harden route-value and verifier models.
- `endpoint_definition_goal_context_policy`: target-vs-analogue, reference
  standard, route demonstration, API, impurity, probe, and process endpoint
  records where the same structure has different acceptable route criteria.
- `reaction_scope_transfer_substrate_class_policy`: substrate-scope transfer,
  functional-group tolerance, substrate class boundaries, and analogue cliff
  records where precedent scope, not reaction type, controls trust.
- `route_diversity_portfolio_and_redundancy_policy`: orthogonal route portfolios,
  backup route value, route diversity, shared bottlenecks, and redundancy records
  where multiple routes are strategically preferable to one optimized route.

## Quality Gates For Every New Expansion File

- All JSON must parse with `python -m json.tool`.
- No duplicate `family_id`, `anchor_id`, or disconnection `id`.
- Every family has at least one anchor and one disconnection.
- Every disconnection has traceable evidence: URL or local file reference.
- Every disconnection has `use_policy` and a `retrosynthetic_move.planner_hint`.
- Regulated, toxic, bioactive-probe, cytotoxic, radiochemical, or hazardous
  precursor entries must carry a `compliance_gate`.
- Run:

```bash
python scripts/audit_strategic_disconnections.py --fail-on-issues
pytest -q tests/test_strategic_disconnections.py
```
